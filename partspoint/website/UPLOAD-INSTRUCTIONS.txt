PARTSPOINT WEBSITE DRAFT - IONOS UPLOAD

1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. In IONOS Webspace Explorer, create a separate empty preview folder.
3. Upload ALL extracted contents into that folder: index.html, assets/, images/, robots.txt, and .htaccess. Do not upload only the ZIP.
4. Open that folder through your preview subdomain or its website path. This package supports both.

Node.js, npm, and WordPress are NOT needed on the hosting account. index.html includes the page content; the supporting files supply styles, fonts, images, and interactions.

CURRENT DRAFT LIMITATIONS
The original website photos, verified Google review text/rating/count, business phone/address/hours, and any real stock/order integration are not yet included. The supplied logo is included with a transparent background. The owner-provided Google profile link is included. The parts enquiry only creates a copyable summary; it does not check availability, send requests, or place orders.

The package has noindex metadata and search exclusions for preview use. It is publicly accessible if uploaded; search exclusions are not password protection. After the final content is approved, prepare a production package and remove the preview search exclusions deliberately.
