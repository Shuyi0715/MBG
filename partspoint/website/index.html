A LITTLE KNOW-HOW HELPS

Good questions.
Clear next steps.

Start with the basics, then bring your appliance details when asking about a part.

What information helps identify the right part?

Start with the appliance brand and complete model number. A part number or a clear photo of the part and its label can also help. Parts can vary between similar-looking appliances, so confirm compatibility before ordering.

Where can I find my model number?

On refrigerators, check the inside wall or door frame. For washers and dryers, look around the door opening or under the lid. Oven labels are often on the door frame; dishwasher labels are often on the inner door edge. Check your owner’s manual for your exact appliance.

Can I identify a part just by how it looks?

Appearance alone is not a reliable compatibility check. Similar parts may have different specifications. Keep the model number and any original part number ready when asking for help.

What if I don’t know which part has failed?

Describe the symptoms and avoid guessing. A diagnosis by a qualified repair professional may be needed before selecting a replacement part.
